Screen change to FFmpeg n9.0.2 (2026-11-20): GIF palettegen's diff mode
must count the incoming pixel, not the pixel in the previous frame.
Without this change a newly introduced final color may be absent. No
video or audio codec was modified. The build applies the patch in an
isolated source copy, leaving the supplied checkout unchanged.

Repository patch: tools/ffmpeg/palettegen-diff-current-frame.patch
